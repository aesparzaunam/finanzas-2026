# PRD: Antigravity Finance App Redesign ## 1. Vision & Goal Create a modern, actionable, and emotional personal finance experience inspired by top-tier fintechs (Revolut, Nubank, Linear). - **Core Improvements:** Enhanced visual hierarchy, high-contrast semantic states, premium dark mode, and clear navigation. ## 2. Design System (Antigravity DS) - **Palette:** - Primary: Electric Blue (#4F7CFF) - Semantic: Income Green (#22C55E), Expense Red (#EF4444), Alert Yellow (#F59E0B) - Neutrals: Background (#0F172A), Card (#111827), Border (#1F2937), Primary Text (#F9FAFB) - **Typography:** Inter / SF Pro (H1: 28px, H2: 22px, Body: 14-16px) - **Components:** 16-20px border radius, glass effects, subtle gradients on primary buttons. ## 3. Screen Roadmap 1. **Home (Inicio):** Hero card with animated gradient, projected balance line chart, and Floating Action Dock. 2. **Accounts (Cuentas):** Bank-card style visualization for accounts with quick balance views. 3. **Budgets (Presupuestos):** Dynamic progress bars (Green/Yellow/Red) based on consumption limits. 4. **MSI (Interest-Free Months):** Wizard-style entry and a clear payment timeline/progress cards. 5. **Transactions (Movimientos):** Feed-style list with category icons and quick-filter chips. ## 4. Navigation - **FAB:** Central "Add Transaction" button. - **Tabs:** Inicio, Cuentas, (+), Historial, Perfil.
